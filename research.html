Research at P-CAIRN has focused on all aspects of patient centered research across a wide range of neurologic diseases. Our previous work has focused on the main theme areas shown here, and we have provided some highlight papers in each theme area.

Patient-centered research

As part of our work with the Comprehensive Longitudinal Investigation of Multiple Sclerosis at Brigham and Women’s Hospital (CLIMB), we have collected a wide range patient reported outcome measures. This has allowed us to understand the MS disease course from the patient perspective as well as differences among MS subtypes.

Patient-centered implementation

Clinical trials

We have piloted several patient centered treatments in MS. We have completed small randomized clinical trials to understand feasibility, acceptability, and initial efficacy for these treatments.

  • Group based positive psychology (PP): Leclaire K et al. Short Report: A Pilot Study of a Group Positive Psychology Intervention for Patients with Multiple Sclerosis. Int J MS Care. 2018 May-Jun;20(3):136-141.

  • At home PP: Freedman ME et al. An At-home Positive Psychology Intervention for Individuals with Multiple Sclerosis: A Phase 1 Randomized Controlled Trial. Int J MS Care. 2021 May-Jun;23(3):128-134.

  • PP in newly diagnosed patients:

  • Barker L et al. A Pilot Study to Assess At-Home Speed of Processing Training for Individuals with Multiple Sclerosis. Mult Scler Int. 2019 Jun 3;2019:3584259.
